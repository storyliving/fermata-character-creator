# Recipe: stylized human

- **Character type:** human
- **Author:** Fermata team
- **Status:** built and uploaded this way ourselves; details vary per character
- **Date:** 2026-10

## The tricky part

Hands, clothes and shoulders. Generated clothes are often one thick shell with an inner and an outer wall, and the arms look broken if the joints or weights are off.

## Parts we split it into

Bald head, hair, torso with garment, two arms, two gloved or bare hands with fingers, legs, shoes, accessories.

## Assembly and rig

- Use a standard humanoid bone naming (Mixamo-style or MetaHuman-style) so the importer recognizes the body and no clips are needed.
- Put the shoulder joints at the shoulder ball, not low.
- Add finger bones to every hand and glove.
- Weld weights across seams and across the inner and outer wall of thick garments.

## Face and emotions

Author the four mouth shapes, the two blinks and the 15 core emotions. Keep the jaw nearly closed in emotions.

## Numbers

Browser hero humans fit in 8,000 to 18,000 triangles, 2048 px textures, 4 influences per vertex.

## Import result

Recognized humanoid: no clips needed. Check the MetaHuman note if you used MetaHuman bone names.
