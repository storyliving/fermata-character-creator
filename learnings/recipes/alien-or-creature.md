# Recipe: alien or creature

- **Character type:** alien, monster, anything unusual
- **Author:** Fermata team
- **Status:** **untested idea** (general technique)
- **Date:** 2026-10

## The tricky part

Unusual anatomy: three eyes, no mouth, tentacles, a body that is one blob.

## How to think about it

Ask: what blinks, what talks, what shows feeling? Map those to the face contract however it makes sense for the anatomy.

- Three eyes: make three eyeballs and let `eyeBlinkLeft` / `eyeBlinkRight` close the left and right ones, with the middle one following your choice (for example, the average).
- No mouth: let `jawOpen` and `mouthFunnel` move a throat sac, a frill, a glowing patch or a body opening. Voice still drives them.
- Tentacles or antennae: put them on their own bone chains and give them idle motion.

## Parts

Whatever moves on its own gets its own plate, mesh and bone.

## Import result

Almost always the non-humanoid path: bring one idle clip.
