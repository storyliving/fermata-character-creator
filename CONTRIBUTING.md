# Share what you learned

This repo is meant to grow into a collective brainstorm of character designs. If you made a character (a dog, a cat, an alien, a bird, a human, something nobody has seen), or found a better way to do one step, please add a recipe. We read them, we fold the good ideas into the skills, and the importer and the character system learn to handle more.

## How

1. Copy [`learnings/TEMPLATE.md`](learnings/TEMPLATE.md) to `learnings/recipes/<short-name>.md`.
2. Fill it in. Short is fine. Say what you tried, what worked, what did not, and how you checked it.
3. Mark anything you did not test as **untested idea**.
4. Open a pull request (or an issue, if you do not use git). A maintainer reviews it.

You can also ask your assistant: *"Turn what we just did into a Fermata recipe."* The builder skill knows the template.

## Good recipes

- Say the character type and the one tricky thing (a long beak, floppy ears, no neck, three eyes).
- List the parts you split it into and why.
- Give the triangle count and what you did to get it under budget.
- Say which face shapes you made and how you made them match the contract.
- Say how it did at upload, and what you had to change.
- Pictures are welcome. Keep images under 2 MB each.

## Hard rules

- **No secrets.** No passwords, tokens, API keys, session links, private URLs, personal data, or private files. Do not paste anything from your logged-in account pages.
- **No other people's assets.** Only characters you made, or have permission to share.
- **No private information about Fermata's internal systems.** Write about how to build and export characters, not how any backend works.
- **Bodies, faces and import only.** Do not post scripts, personalities or private prompts here.
- Be kind. Weird is welcome.

## Bugs in the skills

Open an issue with: what you asked, what the assistant did, what you expected. Remove anything private first.
